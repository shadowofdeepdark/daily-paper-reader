## 摘要
一方说“Fine”，意指“解决”；另一方却听作“投降”。同一个词被共享，但情感理解并不一致。我们将此形式化为<b>情感意义分歧（Affective Meaning Divergence, AMD）</b>，即对话双方的锚点条件情感分布之间的全变差距离。基于言语行为理论、共同基础累积以及熵正则化博弈理论，我们推导出一个逻辑回归最佳响应映射，其动力学经历鞍结分岔：当$βα>4$时，AMD驱动负载的单调增加会导致修复协调的骤然、滞后的崩溃。在“Conversations Gone Awry (CGA-Wiki; $N=652$)”数据集中，脱轨的对话在多个层面上表现出临界慢化（CSD）特征：词汇分歧方差（$p<0.001$, $d=0.36$）、AMD方差（$p=0.001$, $d=0.26$）以及对话行为修复方差（$p=0.016$, $d=0.20$），这些效应在校正后均显著，且强于毒性与情感基线。AMD展现出独特的时间特征——回溯测得的方差在分岔点达到峰值，而毒性方差则更早达到峰值——并且是唯一一个基于理论框架的指标。对CGA-CMV ($N=1{,}169$)的边界条件分析得到混合但在方向上保持一致的证据。

## Abstract
One partner says "Fine" meaning <i>resolution</i>; the other hears <i>surrender</i>. The word is shared; the affective uptake is not. We formalize this as <b>affective meaning divergence (AMD)</b>, the total-variation distance between interlocutors' anchor-conditioned affect distributions. Building on speech-act theory, common-ground accumulation, and entropy-regularized game theory, we derive a logit best-response map whose dynamics undergo a saddle-node bifurcation: when $βα> 4$, a monotone increase in AMD-driven load produces an abrupt, hysteretic collapse of repair coordination. On Conversations Gone Awry (CGA-Wiki; $N=652$), derailing conversations exhibit critical-slowing-down (CSD) signatures across multiple levels: lexical divergence variance ($p<0.001$, $d=0.36$), AMD variance ($p=0.001$, $d=0.26$), and dialog-act repair variance ($p=0.016$, $d=0.20$), all significant after correction and stronger than toxicity and sentiment baselines. AMD provides a distinct temporal signature, with retrospectively measured variance peaking at the bifurcation point while toxicity variance peaks earlier, and is the only indicator grounded in the theoretical framework. Boundary-condition analysis on CGA-CMV ($N=1{,}169$) yields mixed but directionally consistent evidence.
